Stocks and bonds connect your money to the real world, but in completely different ways.
When you buy a stock, you become a small owner of a real company.
When you buy a bond, you become a lender to a company or a government.
As an owner, your fortune rises and falls with the company's success.
As a lender, you simply wait to be repaid, plus a bit of interest.
Owning shares means sharing both the profits and the risks.
Lending through bonds usually feels calmer, but the rewards are smaller too.
Together, they quietly connect ordinary people to the growth of businesses and nations.
