The key difference here is simple, who actually pays you your money.
When you buy a stock, you own a small piece of that company itself.
So any profit the company makes may come back to you as a dividend.
That payment is never guaranteed, it depends on how well the company performs.
When you buy a bond, you are lending money to a government or a company instead.
In return, the borrower promises to pay you fixed interest on a set schedule.
So the stockholder shares in the company's fortune, while the bondholder simply collects what was promised.
