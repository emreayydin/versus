People say they filed their refund. That sentence mixes up two different things.
One is paperwork, the other is money.
A tax return is the form you send to the tax office about your income for the year.
It calculates how much tax you really owed.
A tax refund is money paid back when more tax was withheld than you owed.
If your employer withheld five thousand and you owed four thousand, one thousand comes back.
The difference: the return is the report you file, the refund is the overpayment you get back.
A refund is not a bonus, it is your own money that was held during the year.
It matters because a smaller refund can simply mean less was withheld from each paycheck.
The return is the math. The refund is the change you get back.
