One card is linked to your bank account. The other one isn't linked to anything.
Both look the same at checkout, but what happens behind the scenes is different.
A prepaid card holds a fixed amount of money that you loaded onto it yourself.
Load fifty dollars onto it, and you can spend exactly fifty dollars before it's empty.
A debit card is wired directly into your bank account, checking it in real time.
Spend forty dollars, and your bank balance drops by forty dollars immediately.
The difference is where the money actually sits before you ever spend it.
This matters if a card is lost: a prepaid card can only lose what was loaded on it.
Both pay for coffee the same way. Only one is tied to everything else you own.
