A jackpot of one billion dollars is almost never paid as one billion in cash.
Winners choose between two very different options.
The annuity pays the advertised amount in yearly payments, often over about thirty years.
The lump sum pays a smaller amount immediately, often around half of the headline jackpot.
Both options are then taxed, so the final amount is smaller still.
The difference: the lump sum is less money today, the annuity is more money spread over decades.
The gap exists because money paid later is worth less than money paid now.
The annuity also protects winners from spending everything in a few years.
It matters because many big winners have run into money trouble later.
The lump sum is control now. The annuity is a paycheck for decades.
