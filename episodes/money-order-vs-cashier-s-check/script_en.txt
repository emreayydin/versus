Some landlords will not take a personal check. They ask for one of two other kinds.
Both are paid upfront, so they cannot bounce like a normal check.
A money order is bought at a post office or store, and you fill in who gets paid.
They are meant for small sums: a US postal money order tops out at one thousand dollars.
A cashier's check is issued by a bank and paid from the bank's own funds.
It is used for big payments, like a deposit on a car or a house.
The difference: a money order is a small prepaid payment, a cashier's check is backed by a bank.
Both usually cost a fee, and fake versions of both exist, so the receiver should verify them.
A money order handles the rent. A cashier's check handles the down payment.
