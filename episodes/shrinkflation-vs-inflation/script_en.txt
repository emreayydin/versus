The price did not change. The bag just got smaller.
That is not quite the same thing as inflation.
Inflation is a general rise in prices across the economy, measured over time.
You pay more for the same groceries, rent and services.
Shrinkflation is when one product shrinks while its price stays the same.
A bar shrinks from two hundred grams to one hundred eighty at the same price: eleven percent more per gram.
The difference: inflation raises the price tag, shrinkflation lowers what is inside.
Official price statistics adjust for package size, so shrinkflation still counts as inflation there.
The unit price on the shelf label is the easiest place to spot it.
Inflation changes the number on the tag. Shrinkflation changes the number on the pack.
