One of these banks can create money. The other only moves it around.
They share a name, but they do almost nothing the same.
A central bank sets the interest rate that shapes every loan in the economy.
It has no customers, no branches, no accounts for regular people.
A commercial bank takes your deposits and lends that money out again.
It earns money on the gap between what it pays you and what it charges borrowers.
The difference is one bank serves the public directly, the other steers the whole system.
When interest rates change, the central bank decides it, the commercial bank just passes it on.
One shapes the weather, the other hands you the umbrella.
