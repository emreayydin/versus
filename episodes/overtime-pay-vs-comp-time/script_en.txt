Ten extra hours: some employers pay for them, others give time off.
Those are two different deals.
Overtime pay means extra money for hours beyond the normal limit.
In the US, many hourly workers must get one and a half times their rate after forty hours a week.
Comp time means paid time off later instead of extra money now.
In the US private sector, comp time instead of overtime pay is generally not allowed for those workers.
The difference: overtime pay turns extra hours into money, comp time turns them into future free time.
Public employers and other countries can have different rules.
It matters to know which rule applies to your job before agreeing to extra hours.
Overtime pays you now. Comp time pays you in hours later.
