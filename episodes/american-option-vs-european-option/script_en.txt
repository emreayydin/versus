Two options can have the same strike and date. One can be used much earlier.
The names sound like geography, but they have nothing to do with where you trade.
An option gives you the right to buy or sell at a set price until a set date.
An American option can be exercised on any trading day up to that expiration date.
A European option can only be exercised on the expiration date itself.
In the US, most options on single stocks are American style.
Many options on whole stock indexes are European style.
The difference: American options give flexibility on timing, European options have one fixed moment.
That flexibility can matter, for example just before a stock pays a dividend.
American means any day. European means one day. Neither means a continent.
