One pays rent every month. The other pays whenever it wants.
Both promise to grow your money, but they build wealth in very different ways.
Real estate means owning a physical building, a house or an apartment.
You can live in it, rent it out, or sell it later for more than you paid.
But buying it takes a lot of money upfront, and selling it can take months.
A stock is a tiny slice of a company, bought and sold in seconds.
Its price moves with the company's profits, and you can sell it any day.
You don't fix a stock's roof, but you also don't control what it does.
The real difference is that property gives you control, stocks give you speed.
That difference matters most when you suddenly need your money back fast.
One is slow and heavy, the other is light and quick to move.
