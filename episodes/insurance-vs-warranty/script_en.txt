One pays you when life surprises you. The other pays when a product fails its promise.
Both sound like the same kind of protection, but they are triggered by very different events.
Insurance pools money from many people who each pay a small premium.
That pool pays out when bad luck hits someone, like an accident, illness, or fire.
A warranty is a promise from the seller that the product will work as described.
It only pays out if the product itself is defective, not if you happen to break it.
The difference is what triggers the payout: bad luck for insurance, a broken promise for warranty.
That's why insurance can cover almost anything, while a warranty only ever covers one product.
Both are promises to pay you back, just for completely different reasons.
