A new car can lose a big share of its value before it is even paid off.
That loss is called depreciation, and it explains this whole comparison.
A new car comes with no history, the latest features and a full factory warranty.
Many new cars lose around twenty percent of their value in the first year.
A used car has already taken that first big drop, so the price is lower for the same model.
In return you take on more uncertainty about past care and future repairs.
The difference: with a new car you pay for the steepest depreciation, with a used car the first owner did.
Interest rates and repair costs can shift the math either way.
It matters most when you keep a car for only a few years.
New buys certainty. Used buys a lower price for the same miles ahead.
