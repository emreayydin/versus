Two words spread a cost over time - only one applies to something you can touch.
Both quietly lower a company's profit, but not for the same kind of asset.
A delivery truck costs thirty thousand dollars and loses value every year it drives.
Depreciation spreads that cost over the six years the truck is expected to last.
A patent also costs money, but it never gets flat tires or rust.
Amortization spreads that same kind of cost over the years the patent stays valid.
The difference is what is wearing out: a physical thing or a legal right.
This matters at tax time, since each one lowers taxable profit on its own schedule.
