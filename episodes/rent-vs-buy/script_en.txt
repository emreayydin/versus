One payment disappears every month. The other slowly becomes yours.
But the real difference isn't about the money leaving your account.
Renting buys you flexibility, you can move out with a month's notice.
You never build equity, and the rent can rise with the market.
Buying means a mortgage payment slowly builds ownership over time.
You also pay property tax, maintenance, and interest to the bank.
Prices can fall too, and selling later takes time and costs money.
The real difference is what happens to your money after you pay it.
Renting can be cheaper short term, buying often pays off over decades.
Neither one is right or wrong, they just spread the risk differently.
