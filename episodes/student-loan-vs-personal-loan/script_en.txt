One of these loans can pause itself if you lose your job. The other won't.
Both hand you money today, but they treat your future very differently.
A student loan is built to fund education, often with lower interest rates.
Many student loans let you defer payments while you're still in school.
Some even adjust payments based on how much you earn later.
A personal loan can pay for almost anything, a car, a wedding, a repair.
It's approved faster, but usually comes with a higher interest rate.
Miss a payment, and there's no built-in pause button waiting for you.
The difference is flexibility: one loan bends around your income, the other doesn't.
That difference matters most when life doesn't go as planned, like losing a job.
Same amount borrowed, but very different rules for paying it back.
