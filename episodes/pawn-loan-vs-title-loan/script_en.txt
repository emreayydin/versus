Both give fast cash with no credit check. What you risk losing is very different.
Here is how each one works.
A pawn loan means leaving an item, like jewelry, at a pawn shop as security.
If you do not repay, the shop keeps the item, and the debt usually ends there.
A title loan uses your car as security, while you keep driving it.
Miss payments, and the lender can take the car, which you may need to get to work.
The difference: a pawn loan risks an item you hand over, a title loan risks your car.
Both often come with very high fees compared with regular loans.
It matters to compare the full yearly cost before signing anything.
A pawn loan bets your item. A title loan bets your ride.
