Some economies grow twice as fast. Their markets can swing twice as hard.
That is the basic split between these two groups.
Developed markets are countries like the US, Germany or Japan, with mature economies.
Their institutions, laws and markets are usually stable and deep.
Emerging markets are countries like India, Brazil or Indonesia, still growing fast.
Growth can be higher, but so can political risk and currency swings.
The difference: developed markets offer more stability, emerging markets more growth and more uncertainty.
Which country counts as emerging depends on the index provider.
It matters because a falling local currency can shrink gains for foreign investors.
Developed is the steady road. Emerging is the faster road with more bumps.
