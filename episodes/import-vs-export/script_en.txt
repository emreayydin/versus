One brings goods into a country. The other sends them out.
Both cross borders, but the money moves in opposite directions.
An import is something bought from abroad and brought into the country.
Money leaves the country to pay for it.
An export is something made at home and sold to another country.
Money flows in, because someone abroad is paying for it.
The difference is who receives the money, and who spends it.
When a country imports more than it exports, more money flows out than in.
Trade is simply this exchange, repeated by millions of people every day.
