Two tenants rent the same flat. One can leave next month, the other is tied for a year.
The lease type decides it.
A fixed-term lease runs for a set period, often twelve months.
Rent is usually locked for that time, but leaving early can cost money.
A month-to-month lease renews every month until someone gives notice.
You can leave with short notice, but the landlord can also raise the rent or end it sooner.
The difference: fixed-term trades flexibility for stability, month-to-month trades stability for flexibility.
Month-to-month rent is often a bit higher because of that freedom.
It matters when your plans might change, like a new job or a move.
A fixed term is a promise. Month-to-month is an open door both ways.
