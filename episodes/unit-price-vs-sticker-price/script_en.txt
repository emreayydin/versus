The bigger pack looks like the better deal. The small print on the shelf may disagree.
Two prices sit on the same label.
The sticker price is what you pay at the register for the whole pack.
The unit price shows the cost per ounce, gram or piece.
Sixteen ounces for four dollars is twenty five cents an ounce.
Twenty four ounces for six forty eight is twenty seven cents. The big one costs more per ounce.
The difference: the sticker price is what you pay, the unit price is what you pay for each bit.
Many stores print the unit price on the shelf tag, often in small type.
The sticker price tells you the bill. The unit price tells you the deal.
