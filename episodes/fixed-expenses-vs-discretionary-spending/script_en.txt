Same income, but one always runs out. The split of costs explains it.
Every budget has two kinds of spending.
Fixed expenses are the bills that arrive every month at about the same size: rent, insurance, loans.
You cannot cut them quickly without changing your life.
Discretionary spending is what you choose: eating out, hobbies, streaming, new clothes.
It can change this week, simply by deciding differently.
The difference: fixed expenses are committed in advance, discretionary spending is decided each time.
When fixed costs eat most of the income, there is little room to save at all.
It matters for budgeting, because quick savings come from one side and lasting ones from the other.
Fixed costs are your structure. Discretionary spending is your daily choice.
