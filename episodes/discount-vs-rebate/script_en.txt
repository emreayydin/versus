Twenty dollars off sounds the same either way. When you get it is the catch.
Discount and rebate give the same savings on paper.
A discount lowers the price at the moment you pay.
The product costs eighty instead of a hundred, and that is the end of it.
A rebate means you pay the full price first and get money back later.
Usually you have to send a form, a receipt or a code, often before a deadline.
The difference: a discount is money you never spend, a rebate is money you have to claim back.
Many rebates are never claimed, because people forget or miss the deadline.
It matters when comparing prices, because the rebate price is only real if you follow through.
A discount saves you now. A rebate saves you only if you finish the paperwork.
