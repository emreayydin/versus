Two people pay in parts for the same television. Only one takes it home today.
One plan hands you the item today, the other makes you wait for it.
With layaway, the store holds the item in the back while you make payments.
You take it home only after the last payment, and most stores charge no interest.
Stop paying early and you may lose a small fee, but you owe nothing more.
With an installment plan, the item goes home with you right away.
You now owe the rest of the price, and most plans add interest on top.
Miss a payment and it can show up on your credit report.
The difference is who carries the risk, the store in one case, you in the other.
That risk shows up most clearly the moment a payment gets missed.
One makes you wait to own, the other makes you own and wait to pay.
