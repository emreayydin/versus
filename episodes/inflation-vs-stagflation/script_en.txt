Inflation and Stagflation sound similar. They are not.
Inflation starts with a different question.
Stagflation starts with another question.
Their timing, risk, or purpose can differ.
Inflation can be understood through its own mechanism.
Stagflation follows a different mechanism.
The difference is how Inflation and Stagflation work.
That distinction matters when real situations are compared.
The right label depends on context, not a promise.
