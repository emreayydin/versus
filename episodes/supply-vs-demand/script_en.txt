One side wants to sell for more. The other wants to pay less.
Between those two forces, something invisible ends up setting the price.
Supply is how much sellers are willing to offer at a given price.
When prices rise, sellers usually offer more of it.
Demand is how much buyers are willing to purchase at a given price.
When prices fall, buyers usually want more of it.
The difference is supply rises with price while demand falls with price.
Where the two lines cross, the market settles on one price that clears.
That is why shortages push prices up and surpluses push them down.
Supply and demand don't argue, they just trade until they agree.
