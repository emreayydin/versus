The number on your job offer is never the number that lands in your account.
One is what your employer promises to pay, the other is what survives the deductions.
Gross is the salary written in your contract, before anything gets taken out.
It's the number companies advertise, and the one used to calculate raises.
Net is what actually lands in your bank account after taxes and insurance.
It's smaller, but it's the amount you can actually spend or save.
The difference is everything that gets subtracted before the money becomes yours.
For a gross salary of four thousand dollars, net pay might land around three thousand.
Knowing which number is meant keeps negotiations and budgets from getting confused.
