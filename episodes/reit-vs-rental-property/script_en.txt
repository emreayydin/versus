You can earn rent without fixing a single leaking pipe. You just give up control.
That is the trade between these two.
A rental property means buying a home or apartment and renting it out yourself.
You control it, but you handle tenants, repairs and empty months.
A REIT, a real estate investment trust, is a company that owns many properties and trades like a stock.
In the US, REITs must pay out at least ninety percent of their taxable income to shareholders.
The difference: a rental is one property you run, a REIT is a share in many properties others run.
REITs can be sold in seconds, while selling a house can take months.
It matters for how much time, money and risk you want in one place.
A rental makes you a landlord. A REIT makes you a part owner.
