Two traders sell the same option. One risk is limited, the other has no ceiling.
The only difference is what they already own.
Selling a call option means you collect money now, and promise to sell shares at a set price.
A covered call means you already own those shares.
If the price jumps, you hand over your shares and miss the extra gain, but you lose nothing more.
A naked call means you sold the promise without owning the shares.
If the price jumps, you have to buy at the new price, and in theory there is no limit to how high.
The difference: a covered call caps your upside, a naked call opens unlimited loss.
That is why brokers allow naked calls only for approved accounts with strict margin rules.
A covered call trades away some gain. A naked call can take far more than you collected.
