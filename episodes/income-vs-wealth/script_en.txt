One person earns more each month. The other owns more overall.
Those sound the same, but they measure completely different things.
Income is what flows in over time, like a paycheck every month.
A high income can disappear the moment the job stops.
Wealth is what's left when you add up everything you own and subtract what you owe.
A house, savings, and investments all count, even without a paycheck.
The difference is income measures flow, wealth measures what's already been built.
This is why someone with a modest salary can still be wealthier than a high earner.
Income pays today's bills, wealth is what remains once the bills are paid.
