Owning fifty tech stocks is diversified, sort of. It is still one bet on one sector.
That is where two ideas separate.
Asset allocation is how you split money between big categories like stocks, bonds and cash.
It sets most of the overall risk of a portfolio.
Diversification is spreading money within a category, across many companies, sectors or countries.
It reduces the damage when one company or sector has a bad year.
The difference: asset allocation decides the big mix, diversification spreads risk inside each part.
You can be diversified within stocks and still have a very risky allocation.
It matters because the two solve different problems.
Allocation chooses the ingredients. Diversification makes sure no single one ruins the meal.
