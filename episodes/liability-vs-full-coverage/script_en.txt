After a crash, some learn their policy pays for the other car, not theirs.
That is the gap between these two policies.
Liability insurance pays for damage and injuries you cause to other people.
In most US states, a minimum amount of it is required by law.
Full coverage usually adds collision and comprehensive insurance.
That covers your own car after a crash, theft, a storm or a falling tree.
The difference: liability protects other people from you, full coverage also protects your own car.
Full coverage costs more, and lenders often require it while a car is financed.
It matters most for valuable cars, and less for old ones worth little.
Liability is the legal minimum. Full coverage is protection for your own wheels too.
