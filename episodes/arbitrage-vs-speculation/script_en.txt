Two traders both buy gold. One already knows the profit, the other is hoping.
That is the line between two words people mix up all the time.
Arbitrage means buying and selling the same thing at the same time in two places.
Gold costs one thousand in one market and one thousand and five in another. Buy low, sell high, at once.
Speculation means buying because you expect the price to move later.
If the price rises, you win. If it falls, you lose. Nothing is locked in.
The difference: arbitrage uses a price gap that exists now, speculation bets on a price that may come.
Real arbitrage gaps are tiny and close fast, because computers hunt for them all day.
Arbitrage profits from a gap. Speculation profits only if the guess is right.
