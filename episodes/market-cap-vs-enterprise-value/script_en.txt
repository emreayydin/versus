One price tag ignores debt. The other counts every cent of it.
Both claim to show what a company is worth, but they measure different things.
Market cap is the share price times the number of shares outstanding.
A stock at fifty dollars with twenty million shares gives a market cap of one billion.
Enterprise value starts from market cap, then adds debt and subtracts cash.
With that debt and cash, enterprise value rises to one billion, one hundred fifty million.
The difference is market cap prices the shares, enterprise value prices the whole business.
This matters most when comparing companies that carry very different amounts of debt.
Same company, two numbers, two different questions about what owning it really costs.
