Three thousand dollars more this year. One version keeps paying, one does not.
It depends on whether it is a bonus or a raise.
A raise increases your base pay, so the extra money comes back every year.
Future raises in percent are then calculated on the higher salary.
A bonus is a one time payment, often tied to results or the company's year.
Next year, your pay starts from the old number again.
Over five years, a three thousand dollar raise adds up to fifteen thousand. A single bonus stays at three.
The difference: a raise changes your salary for good, a bonus changes only one paycheck.
That is why companies often prefer paying bonuses. They are easier to stop.
A bonus is a gift for one year. A raise is a new starting point.
