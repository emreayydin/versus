One payment shrinks your loan. The other just sits in a locked account.
Both show up at the same closing, but they do completely different jobs.
A down payment is money you pay upfront, straight toward the price of the house.
Put forty thousand down on a two hundred thousand dollar house, and you only borrow one sixty.
That money becomes equity, immediately yours in the property.
Escrow is money a third party holds, not spent yet, just kept safe.
During the sale it holds your deposit until the deal actually closes.
After you own the house, it collects a slice of your payment each month for taxes and insurance.
The difference: a down payment buys you ownership, escrow just holds money for later.
This matters at closing, when both numbers sit on the same page and look almost identical.
Same closing table, two very different pockets.
