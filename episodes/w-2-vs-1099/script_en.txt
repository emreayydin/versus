Two people do the same job for the same pay. One of them owes a lot more tax in April.
The form they get decides it.
A W-2 employee has taxes taken out of every paycheck by the employer.
The employer also pays half of Social Security and Medicare taxes for them.
A 1099 contractor is paid the full amount, with no taxes taken out.
They must pay both halves of those taxes themselves, often in quarterly payments.
The difference: W-2 means the employer handles part of the taxes, 1099 means you handle all of it.
In return, contractors can usually deduct more business expenses.
It matters when comparing job offers, because the same number is not the same pay.
A W-2 is a job with a safety net. A 1099 is a business of one.
