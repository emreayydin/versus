Split a purchase into four payments, or put it on a card. Both feel like paying later.
The costs work very differently.
Buy-now-pay-later splits one purchase into a few fixed payments, often with no interest.
Miss a payment, though, and late fees can follow.
A credit card gives you a credit line you can use again and again.
Pay the full balance each month and there is usually no interest. Carry it, and interest adds up fast.
The difference: buy-now-pay-later is one plan for one purchase, a credit card is an open line for everything.
Several small plans at once are easy to lose track of.
It matters for your budget, because both make spending feel smaller than it is.
One splits a single bill. The other keeps a tab open.
