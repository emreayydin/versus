A TV costs five hundred, or a weekly rent that adds up to much more.
That is the core of rent-to-own.
Buying means paying the price, with cash or a loan, and owning the item right away.
Rent-to-own means weekly or monthly payments, and you own it only after the last one.
Over time, the total can reach double the store price or more.
You can usually return it and stop, but the payments made are gone.
The difference: buying means owning now, rent-to-own means paying extra for flexibility and no credit check.
For homes, rent-to-own deals exist too, with their own contracts and risks.
It matters to add up all payments before agreeing.
Buying is the price. Rent-to-own is the price plus a lot of patience.
