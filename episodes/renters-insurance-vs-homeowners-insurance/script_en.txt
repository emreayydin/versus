A fire damages an apartment building. Whose insurance pays for what?
It depends on which of two policies you have.
Renters insurance covers your belongings inside the home, not the building itself.
It usually also covers liability, if someone is hurt in your place and blames you.
The building belongs to the landlord, so the landlord insures the walls and roof.
Homeowners insurance covers the structure, plus your belongings and liability in one policy.
The difference: renters insurance protects what you own inside, homeowners insurance also protects the house.
That is why renters insurance usually costs far less each month.
It matters after a loss, because a renter without a policy often has to replace everything alone.
Renters insurance covers your stuff. Homeowners insurance covers your stuff and the walls around it.
