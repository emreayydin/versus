Your banking app shows two balances. Spend the wrong one and you can overdraw.
They come from the same account but count different things.
The current balance shows the money after all transactions that have fully posted.
The available balance also subtracts pending payments and holds that have not posted yet.
Say your current balance is five hundred dollars.
A pending card payment of eighty and a hotel hold of fifty leave three hundred seventy available.
The difference: the current balance looks back, the available balance includes what is about to leave.
Checks you deposit can also show in one balance before they count in the other.
The current balance is the past. The available balance is what you can actually use now.
