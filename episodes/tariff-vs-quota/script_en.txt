One makes foreign goods cost more. The other simply says how many are allowed.
Both are meant to slow down imports, but they do it in opposite ways.
A tariff is a tax on imported goods, paid at the border.
It raises the price of foreign goods, so buyers often switch to local ones.
A quota sets a hard limit on how many units of a good can enter the country.
Once that number is reached, no more come in, no matter what price people offer.
The difference is that a tariff changes the price, a quota changes the quantity.
This matters when demand jumps, a tariff still lets more in at a higher price.
Same goal, protecting local industry, reached through price or through quantity.
