Your credit score is built from several pieces. Two of them carry most of the weight.
They measure very different habits.
Payment history shows whether you paid your bills on time, month after month.
In the FICO model, it is the largest factor, about thirty-five percent.
Credit utilization is how much of your available credit you are using right now.
A two thousand balance on a ten thousand limit is twenty percent utilization.
The difference: payment history looks back at reliability, utilization looks at how stretched you are today.
A late payment can hurt for years, while utilization can improve as soon as balances drop.
It matters before applying for a loan, when a lower balance can help quickly.
Payment history is your track record. Utilization is your current load.
