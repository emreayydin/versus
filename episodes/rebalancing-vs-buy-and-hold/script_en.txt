You start half stocks, half bonds. Years later it is eighty percent stocks.
What you do next has a name.
Buy and hold means keeping what you bought and letting it grow, whatever the weights become.
Rebalancing means returning to your target mix by selling some winners and adding to the rest.
A mix that drifted from fifty to eighty percent stocks carries much more risk than planned.
Rebalancing brings it back to the level of risk you chose.
The difference: buy and hold lets the mix drift, rebalancing keeps the mix on target.
Selling can trigger taxes and fees, which is part of the trade-off.
It matters most after big market moves.
Buy and hold leaves the garden alone. Rebalancing trims it back into shape.
