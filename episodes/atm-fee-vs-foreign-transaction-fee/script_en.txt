On vacation, one card swipe can cost you twice: once at the machine and once at your bank.
These are two separate fees.
An ATM fee is charged for using a cash machine, by its owner, your bank, or both.
It is often a flat amount per withdrawal, like three to five dollars.
A foreign transaction fee is a percentage your card charges on purchases in another currency.
A three percent fee on a two thousand dollar trip adds sixty dollars.
The difference: an ATM fee is per withdrawal, a foreign fee is a cut of every foreign payment.
Some cards charge neither, so it pays to check before traveling.
It matters most on long trips, where small fees add up.
One fee charges per visit to the machine. The other charges on every swipe abroad.
