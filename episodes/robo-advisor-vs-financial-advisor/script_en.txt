One manages money with an algorithm for a small fee. The other is a person you can call.
Here is what each one actually does.
A robo-advisor is an online service that builds and rebalances a portfolio automatically.
It usually charges a low yearly fee, often well under one percent.
A financial advisor is a person who can also help with taxes, retirement or estate planning.
Personal advice often costs more, through fees or commissions.
The difference: a robo-advisor automates one portfolio, a financial advisor can look at your whole situation.
Some advisors are fiduciaries, meaning they must act in the client's best interest.
It matters to ask how someone is paid, because that can shape the advice.
A robo is a smart autopilot. An advisor is a co-pilot you can talk to.
