Both pay medical bills with untaxed money. Only one lets you keep it.
That one rule changes how you use them.
An HSA, a health savings account, is only available with a high deductible health plan.
The money stays yours year after year, even if you change jobs.
An FSA, a flexible spending account, is offered through an employer.
Unused FSA money is often lost at the end of the plan year, apart from limited carryover rules.
The difference: HSA money rolls over and belongs to you, FSA money is usually use it or lose it.
Contribution limits and rules are set each year, so the numbers change.
It matters when choosing how much to put in, especially for an FSA.
An HSA is a health savings jar. An FSA is a yearly health budget.
