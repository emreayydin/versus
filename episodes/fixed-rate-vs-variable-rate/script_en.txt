One rate never moves. The other moves with the market.
Both decide what a loan actually ends up costing you over time.
A fixed rate locks in one number for the whole loan term.
Your payment stays the same whether the market rate rises or falls.
A variable rate follows a benchmark set by the central bank.
When that benchmark moves, your payment moves right along with it.
The real difference is who carries the risk of rates changing.
That risk shows up the moment interest rates start climbing or falling.
Fixed buys certainty, variable bets on where rates go next.
