One keeps you fed and housed. The other just makes life more fun.
Economists split spending into these two boxes for a reason.
A need is something you can't function without, like food or rent.
Cut a need and your life gets harder immediately, not eventually.
A want is something that makes life better, but you'd survive without it.
A new phone, a vacation, a nicer car - all wants, no matter how strong they feel.
The difference is not the price tag, it's what happens if you don't buy it.
That line matters most when money runs short and choices have to be made.
Budgets that ignore this line usually run out before the month does.
Needs keep you standing. Wants decide how good that standing feels.
