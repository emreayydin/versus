Two people drive the same new car for the same monthly payment. Only one will own it.
Leasing and financing work in opposite directions.
When you lease, you pay for the part of the car's value that is used up while you drive it.
At the end, you give it back, and there are usually limits on how many miles you can drive.
When you finance, you take a loan to buy the whole car and pay it off over time.
Once the loan is paid, the car is yours, with no mileage limit.
The difference: leasing is paying to use the car, financing is paying to own it.
Lease payments are often lower, because you do not pay for the car's full value.
It matters for how long you keep cars, and how far you drive them.
A lease is a long rental. Financing is a purchase on installments.
