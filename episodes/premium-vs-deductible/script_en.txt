A cheap insurance plan can cost more than a pricey one. Two words explain it.
You pay both, but at different times.
The premium is the regular price of the policy, paid monthly or yearly.
You pay it whether you make a claim or not.
The deductible is what you pay yourself before the insurance starts paying for a claim.
With a one thousand dollar deductible, a fifteen hundred dollar repair costs you one thousand.
The difference: the premium is the cost of having insurance, the deductible is your share when you use it.
A higher deductible usually means a lower premium, and the other way around.
It matters to check whether you could pay the deductible tomorrow if you had to.
The premium is the subscription. The deductible is the first part of every bill.
