The store will not give your money back. Your card company might.
Getting money back can happen in two very different ways.
A refund is when the seller agrees and sends the money back to your card.
You return the shoes, the shop reverses the charge, and that is it.
A chargeback is when you dispute the charge with your card issuer instead.
The issuer looks at the case and can pull the money back from the seller's bank.
The difference: a refund comes from the seller, a chargeback is forced through your bank.
Chargebacks are meant for fraud, items never delivered, or charges that are wrong.
There are deadlines, often a few months, and the seller can fight it.
A refund is the seller saying yes. A chargeback is asking someone else to decide.
