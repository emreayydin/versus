One explains why you buy coffee. The other explains why prices rise everywhere.
Both are economics, but they zoom to very different levels.
Microeconomics looks at single choices, one buyer, one seller, one price.
It explains why a cup of coffee costs three dollars and not five.
It studies supply, demand, and how one company sets its price.
Macroeconomics looks at the whole economy, not one shop.
It explains why prices rise everywhere at once, called inflation.
It tracks things like unemployment, growth, and interest rates set by central banks.
The difference is scale, one studies a single market, the other studies the whole economy.
A business owner uses micro to price a product, a government uses macro to set policy.
Together they explain both your grocery bill and the whole economy's health.
