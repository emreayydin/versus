Two people sign the exact same contract. One is reducing risk, the other is seeking it.
The contract is identical. The intention is not.
Hedging means taking a position that protects you against a risk you already have.
A wheat farmer can lock in today's price for the harvest, so a price crash does not ruin the year.
Speculation means taking on risk on purpose, hoping to profit from a price move.
A trader with no farm can make the same bet on wheat, simply to profit if prices move his way.
The difference: a hedger is trying to lose less, a speculator is trying to win more.
Markets need both, because the speculator often takes the risk the hedger wants to get rid of.
It matters for judging a trade, because the same position can be careful or risky.
Hedging is insurance against a move. Speculation is a bet on a move.
