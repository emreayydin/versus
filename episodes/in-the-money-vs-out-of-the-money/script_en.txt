Two options on the same stock. One already has real value, the other is pure hope.
Traders describe this with three little words.
An option is in the money when using it today would be worth something.
A call to buy at ninety while the stock trades at one hundred is ten dollars in the money.
An option is out of the money when using it today would be pointless.
A call to buy at one hundred twenty, with the stock at one hundred, has no value right now.
The difference: in the money options have value today, out of the money options only have time.
Out of the money options are cheaper, but many expire worthless.
It matters because the price of an option is part real value and part time value.
In the money is worth something now. Out of the money is a bet on what happens later.
