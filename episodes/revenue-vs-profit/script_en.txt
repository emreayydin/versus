One number is what comes in. The other is what's left.
Both numbers sit in the same earnings report, but they answer different questions.
Revenue is the total money a company earns from selling its products or services.
It says nothing about how much of that money the company actually keeps.
Profit is what remains after every cost is subtracted from that revenue.
A company can earn a million dollars in revenue and keep only fifty thousand in profit.
The difference is that revenue shows size, while profit shows what's actually earned.
A business can grow its revenue for years and still lose money if profit stays negative.
Knowing which number you're looking at shows what a company's success really looks like.
