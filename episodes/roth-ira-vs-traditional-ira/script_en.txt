One taxes your money now. The other taxes it later.
Both are retirement accounts, but the timing of taxes changes everything.
With a traditional IRA, you deduct contributions from your taxable income today.
The money then grows without any taxes taken out each year.
Every dollar you withdraw in retirement counts as ordinary income.
With a Roth IRA, you pay income tax on the money before it goes in.
It then grows completely tax-free for as long as it stays invested.
Withdrawals in retirement come out with no tax at all.
The difference is simply when the tax bill comes due, now or later.
That choice matters most if you expect your tax rate to change over time.
Both can build a retirement fund, they just collect the tax at different times.
