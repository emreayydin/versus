A business can report a profit and still not have enough cash to pay rent.
The reason lies in timing, not in how much money a business truly earns.
Profit is what remains after subtracting all costs from revenue on paper.
It counts a sale the moment it happens, even before the customer pays.
Cash flow tracks the money that actually moves through a bank account.
It ignores invoices sent and counts only money that has truly arrived.
A company can show fifty thousand dollars in profit while its cash sits at zero.
The difference is timing: profit is earned, cash flow is what you can spend today.
This gap explains why profitable businesses can still run out of money.
Profit shows how well a business performs, cash flow shows if it can survive.
