A US price tag leaves the tax out. A European one already includes it.
Sales tax and value added tax collect money in very different ways.
Sales tax is charged once, when the final customer buys the product in a store.
In the US it is set by states and cities, and there is no national sales tax.
VAT, or value added tax, is charged at every step from maker to shop to customer.
At twenty percent, a shop buys goods for one hundred plus twenty tax and sells them for one fifty plus thirty.
It hands over only ten, because the twenty it already paid is credited back.
The difference: sales tax is collected once at the end, VAT is collected in pieces along the way.
In both cases the final customer pays the full tax, here thirty on one hundred fifty.
Sales tax is one bite at the register. VAT is many small bites that add up to the same.
