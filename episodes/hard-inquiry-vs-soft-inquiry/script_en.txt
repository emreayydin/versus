Two people looked at your credit today. Only one of them can cost you points.
It depends on why they looked.
A hard inquiry happens when you apply for credit, like a card, a car loan or a mortgage.
It can lower your score by a few points and stays on your report for about two years.
A soft inquiry happens when you check your own score or a company sends a pre-screened offer.
Soft inquiries do not affect your credit score at all.
The difference: a hard inquiry comes from a credit application, a soft inquiry is just a look.
It matters when you shop around, because many applications in a row add up.
Checking your own score is always safe. Applying is what leaves a mark.
