Buy a share and sell it a second later. You already lost money. Here is why.
Every price on a screen is really two prices.
The bid is the highest price a buyer is currently willing to pay.
The ask is the lowest price a seller is currently willing to accept.
If the bid is ninety nine dollars ninety and the ask is a hundred ten, the gap is twenty cents.
You buy at the ask and sell at the bid, so a round trip right away costs you that gap.
The difference: the bid is what you get when you sell, the ask is what you pay when you buy.
The gap is called the spread, and it is wider for things that are traded rarely.
It matters for frequent trading, where small spreads add up to real costs.
The bid is the buyer's price. The ask is the seller's price. The spread lives in between.
