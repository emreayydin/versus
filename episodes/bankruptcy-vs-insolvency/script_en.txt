People use these two words like they mean the same thing. Courts don't.
One is a financial state. The other is a legal process to deal with it.
Insolvency means you cannot pay your debts as they come due.
It describes a condition, not a filing, and it can sometimes be temporary.
Bankruptcy is a legal process, filed in court, to resolve that inability to pay.
A court gets involved, debts are restructured or discharged, and assets may be sold.
The difference is insolvency is the problem, bankruptcy is one legal answer to it.
A company can stay insolvent for months and fix it quietly, never filing for bankruptcy.
Every bankruptcy involves insolvency, but not every insolvency ends in bankruptcy.
