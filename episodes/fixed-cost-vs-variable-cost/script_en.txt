One bill arrives no matter what you do. The other grows with every choice you make.
Businesses live and die by knowing which is which.
Rent, insurance, a manager's salary - these stay the same every month.
Whether you sell one item or a thousand, that number does not move.
This gives you a stable number to plan around.
Raw materials, packaging, shipping - these rise and fall with output.
Make more, spend more. Make nothing, spend nothing.
This makes your costs flexible, but harder to predict exactly.
The difference is what happens to the cost when your output changes.
Fixed costs need volume to survive. Variable costs let a business shrink safely.
Knowing which is which tells you where the real risk in a business sits.
