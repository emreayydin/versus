Per ride or per month: the gap is bigger than the hourly rate.
Here is what each one really includes.
Gig work is paid per task, with no fixed hours and no promise of the next job.
You choose when to work, but you usually cover your own costs, like fuel and phone.
A full-time job pays a set wage and often adds benefits like paid leave and health coverage.
Those benefits can be worth a large share of the salary on top.
The difference: gig work trades stability for flexibility, a full-time job trades flexibility for stability.
Comparing only the hourly number leaves out costs, benefits and quiet weeks.
It matters most when gig income is the only income, not just extra.
Gig work is freedom with risk. A full-time job is security with a schedule.
