One tax knows your name. The other hides in the price tag.
Both come out of your pocket, but they take very different routes to get there.
A direct tax is charged straight to you, based on your income or your assets.
The tax office sends you the bill, and you pay it directly, no middleman.
An indirect tax is baked into the price before you ever reach the register.
The shop collects it for the government and simply passes it along.
The difference is that direct tax follows the earner, indirect tax follows the purchase.
That split decides who notices first when a tax rate goes up.
Same government, same money, just two different ways of collecting it.
